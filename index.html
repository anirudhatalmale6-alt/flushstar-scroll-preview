Quantum 25  /  Automatic Marine Flushing System

Cleaner engines.
Longer life.

01  /  The problem

Salt never stops working.

Saltwater quietly wears down every cooling passage in your outboard. Every run leaves salt, sand and mineral buildup behind.

02  /  The system

One manifold. Up to six engines.

12VDC Plug n Play. Runs off a standard garden hose and feeds every engine from a single supply.

03  /  The cycle

One push. Engine stays off.

Pulsating and continuous water flow clears the full cooling circuit, one engine after another. No hose-holding. No ear muffs. No guesswork.
